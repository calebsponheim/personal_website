Lemon is the institutional AI agent for NN/G, built by me. It's simultaneously an experiment and a load-bearing piece of production automation software. It's at the same time a purposeful dip into AI psychosis and a series of tightly scoped, audited, responsible tools that help workers save time and money. It's my playground and a python codebase.

Lemon is also my first foray into ai-assisted programming and software development. I shudder to call it vibe-coding due to its connotations, but that's truly what it is. I don't read *every* diff, but (if you'll excuse me for being defensive) I know every product decision, every sub-agent, every doc that's written.

Lemon is a conversational information-seeking agent, as well as a collection of automated, segmented subagents responsible for automating workflows and pipelines throughout the organization.

These subagents proofread content, ideate social media posts, evaluate request tickets and provide feedback, post about upcoming design work, transcribe videos to provide captions, create metadata for our CMS, and so on and so forth. It doesn't matter if NN/G employees never chat with Lemon, it's helping make their work better regardless. The list grows every week.

Lemon is an opportunity for NN/G as an organization to dogfood our own best practices. When designing an AI experience, what SHOULD it look like? How SHOULD it act? What problems SHOULD it solve? You can read more about Lemon on the blog.
