My interest in design training goes back further than NN/G. At Grinnell College, I was Design Lead and one of only two active designers at Grinnell AppDev, a student organization that already had a training track for developers but nothing equivalent for designers. So I built one: a six-week curriculum grounded in conceptual literature — human-computer interaction, designing for the screen, iOS design guidelines, Material Design guidelines — followed by a series of projects that taught trainees the AppDev design workflow while giving me a chance to evaluate each person's skills as they progressed. The program ended with a working prototype, refined through informal usability testing.

That instinct to turn what I'd learned into something teachable followed me into my career. I've done a lot while at NN/G, but here's a non-exhaustive list:

- Designed, built, and launched NN/G's first course on AI, Practical AI for UX Professionals, becoming NN/G's first AI SME in the process
- Designed, built, and launched NN/G's second course on AI, Designing AI Experiences, which has been NN/G's most popular course since launch in Spring 2025.
- Developed and helped launch a self-paced course as part of the NN/G self-paced beta in late 2025
- Developed and helped launch a second self-paced course in Summer 2026
- Won third place speaker prize for my talk at WaysConf 2025 in Krakow, Poland
- Written myriad articles and recorded myriad youtube videos on various UX topics
