I develop live and self-paced courses at NN/G. I teach AI design, UX research, and statistics.

## Designing AI Experiences

In [Designing AI Experiences](https://www.nngroup.com/courses/designing-ai-experiences/), we compare examples of AI interfaces and work out what each one needs to tell the user about what the model can do. Participants consider how the interface should respond when the model falls short.

## Bringing research into the classroom

In my research courses, I teach people to interpret data and decide where AI might help with their own work. [PROVE](/projects/prove/) gives them a way to test a tool on a task they already know how to do.

## Speaking and writing

I’ve spoken about AI at WaysConf in Krakow and UX on the Beach in Venice. I also write articles and record videos for NN/G. My [WaysConf talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=guGAfmV_NSM) explores the problems with humanizing AI.

You can find more of my [articles, videos, and courses at NN/G](https://www.nngroup.com/people/caleb-sponheim/).
