# About

Hi, I'm Caleb.

I'm a Senior Experience Specialist and the AI Lead at [NN/G](https://www.nngroup.com/people/caleb-sponheim/). I study AI to understand how best to use it, and how best to design high quality AI experiences.

## AI work

I lead AI initiatives at NN/G, which entails teaching NN/G's flagship AI courses like [Designing AI Experiences](https://www.nngroup.com/courses/designing-ai-experiences/) and running our institutional AI agent and automation platform, Lemon.

I am in charge of internal AI education, enablement, and deployment at NN/G.

I also give talks, create self-paced courses, write articles, and publish videos on myriad topics through NN/G.

## Before NN/G

I have a PhD in computational neuroscience from the University of Chicago. My [research](/projects/neuroscience-research/) involved brain–machine interfaces, neural recordings, and how brain activity relates to movement. I spent a lot of time working with uncertain evidence and explaining findings to people with very different expertise.

I'm also a theater kid and an IT nerd. I've rigged lighting, built [interactive art](/projects/interactive-art/), worked helpdesk support, assembled video kits, and made a [podcast about being a scientist](/projects/science-and-feelings/). At any stage in my life, I've tried my best to be the person in the room who volunteers to help.

## What matters to me

I use AI every day, and I care about its labor, environmental, and societal implications. Anyone using a AI should be able to understand its limits, question an answer, and retain control over consequential decisions.

I also care about the infrastructure behind these tools. Data centers should run on clean energy, and the people who live around them should have a say in what gets built.

I've written and spoken about AI, UX, and statistics, and been quoted in [TIME](https://time.com/7346052/problem-ai-flattering-us/), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/07/07/ai-sycophancy-chatbots-mental-health), [CNET](https://www.cnet.com/tech/services-and-software/apples-genmoji-might-be-a-prompt-engineering-training-tool-in-disguise/), and [Futurism](https://futurism.com/sycophancy-chatbots-ai-problem). You can explore my [work](/projects/) or [get in touch](mailto:caleb.sponheim@gmail.com).
