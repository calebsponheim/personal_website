# About

Hi! I'm an AI expert that teaches designers, researchers, and organizations how to use and design AI responsibly. I write and speak about AI, XR, statistics; I also help my organization integrate AI into processes and workflows.

Being a practical and pragmatic AI expert means maintaining some tensions. AI is impactful and powerful; tech companies have also made some of the most destructive decisions in human history.

I use AI every day to accelerate and improve my work; data centers should also never be powered by fossil fuels and should stay out of residential neighborhoods.

I'm a theater kid and an IT nerd, and I've taught thousands of people how to design and use technology responsibly and respectfully.

I have a bundle of skills that pull together to represent... a person with a love of technology. I've rigged lighting, built interactive art, worked helpdesk, assembled video kits, and generally been the person in the room who volunteers to help fix things.

On paper, I have a PhD in computational neuroscience from the University of Chicago, and I'm now a Senior Experience Specialist and AI Lead at [NN/G](https://www.nngroup.com/people/caleb-sponheim/), where I help folks make practical decisions about designing good products. I've been cited as an expert on AI in [Time](https://time.com/7346052/problem-ai-flattering-us/), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/07/07/ai-sycophancy-chatbots-mental-health), [Futurism](https://futurism.com/sycophancy-chatbots-ai-problem), and [CNET](https://www.cnet.com/tech/services-and-software/apples-genmoji-might-be-a-prompt-engineering-training-tool-in-disguise/), mostly for saying that sycophantic chatbots aren't a great product proposition.
