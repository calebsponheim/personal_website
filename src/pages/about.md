# About

I'm a former neuroscientist, which means I know a lot about the brain, but also that I carry a lot of guilt and trauma around, due to grad school. 

I'm an AI expert who thinks that AI models are impactful and powerful; I also think that tech companies have made some of the most destructive decisions in human history.

I use AI every day to accelerate and improve my work, while also strongly believing that data centers should never be powered by fossil fuels and should stay out of residential neighborhoods.

Growing up, I learned that I could emotionally express myself on the stage, but not anywhere else. Now, I've taught thousands of people how to design and use technology responsibly and respectfully using those same skills.

I have a bundle of skills that pull together to represent... a person with an anxiety disorder and a love of technology. I've rigged lighting, built interactive art, worked helpdesk, assembled video kits, and generally been the person in the room who volunteers for things.

So, on paper, I look like a PhD in computational neuroscience at the University of Chicago, and now a Senior Experience Specialist and AI Lead at [NN/G](https://www.nngroup.com/people/caleb-sponheim/), where I help folks make practical decisions about designing good products. I've been cited as an expert on AI in [Time](https://time.com/7346052/problem-ai-flattering-us/), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/07/07/ai-sycophancy-chatbots-mental-health), [Futurism](https://futurism.com/sycophancy-chatbots-ai-problem), and [CNET](https://www.cnet.com/tech/services-and-software/apples-genmoji-might-be-a-prompt-engineering-training-tool-in-disguise/), mostly for saying that a chatbot flattering you all day might not be doing you any favors.
